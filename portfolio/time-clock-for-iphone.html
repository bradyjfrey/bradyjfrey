Time Clock for iPhone

Bently Holdings

Client Launch: July 2011
What I did
UI Design
UX Design
Art Direction

To provide a more refined mobile experience to Bently Holdings multiple users across various states, I designed a heavily customized iPhone application to merge and unify the native web application.

Syncing in real time with the web application, the design customizes all aspect of the native iOS UI. The navigation bar, the title bar, and all points of notifications and structure have a unified design that reflects in the Time Clock web application. The pay off for branding is additional time to customize default areas of design Apple heavily locks down.
